HOW IT WORKS / EXCHANGE

          
A conversation,

          [ ask ]
          
One question at a time

          
Large type and large buttons, so it works across a noisy counter. Staff answer Yes, No or Unsure. Hand the phone over, or keep holding it.

        

      


      

        

          

            
YOU -> KITCHEN -> YOU

            

          

        

        

          [ understand ]
          
See what is confirmed

          
A plain summary in your language: what they told you, and what still needs a word with the chef. Unsure counts as unknown, never as safe.

          
(!)
Celio does not decide whether a meal is safe. It helps two people understand each other.

          [ send ahead ]
          
Say it before you arrive
